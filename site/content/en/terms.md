# Terms of use

Last updated: 21 September 2026.

## What MathTrail is

A free app for Claude and ChatGPT that gives a child in grades 1–6 a stream of olympiad maths tasks. It is free, and it stays free.

It is not a homework solver and not a drill of a school syllabus, and it is not a replacement for a teacher.

## Who may use it

An adult — a parent or a tutor — who is old enough to use the chat platform they are using. Claude and ChatGPT each set their own age rules, and those apply to you first. The child does not sign in and has no account.

**The adult runs the lesson.** You open the task, read it with the child if they need it, and decide when to stop. MathTrail chooses the topic and the difficulty; it does not supervise anybody.

## What "checked" means

Every task is written by the chat's own model and then checked by a program before the child sees it. That check is exact and it is also limited. It verifies that:

- exactly one of the options is correct;
- a solver program reproduces that answer by brute force;
- the wording is readable for the grade;
- the task is not a near-duplicate of an earlier one;
- any text drawing follows the required format.

Whether the wording, the drawing and the solution agree **in meaning** is checked by the model itself, not by the program. So MathTrail does not promise that every task is flawless, and you should not treat it as a source of authoritative answers. If a task looks wrong, it may be wrong — tell us.

Limits apply so that one user cannot exhaust the service for everyone: a cap on how quickly requests may arrive and a cap on tasks per day. The numbers may change.

Access can be cut off if it is used this way.
