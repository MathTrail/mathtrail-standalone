Why not just ask the chat?

Ask a chat model for “an olympiad task for grade 2” and it will cheerfully hand you a task with no solution, with two correct options, or with the arithmetic wrong. MathTrail leaves the writing to the model and puts a program behind it.

01

Tasks do not run out.

Every task is written for the child's topic, difficulty, interests and yesterday's mistake, in the language of the chat — not drawn from a fixed bank in one language.

02

A program checks the model.

A task reaches the child only after it passes the checks: exactly one correct option, the answer reproduced by a brute-force solver, readability for the grade, no near-duplicate of an earlier task, a well-formed text drawing.

03

A wrong answer is a diagnosis.

Every wrong option is tied to a named trap — off-by-one in gaps, a missed case while enumerating, double counting — and the explanation starts from how the child reasoned, not from the right answer.

For the parent

You stay in charge.

Before

Set up a profile

A made-up name, the grade, interests to set the stories in, and what school hasn’t covered yet — say, division with remainder.

During

Sit alongside

You run the lesson: read the task aloud, ask a leading question, or ask the chat for one. A daily limit on new tasks keeps the free service free for everyone.

After

Read the progress

Ratings by topic, mastered topics, recent answers and the mistakes that repeat — the same screen your child sees.

One thing we don’t hide: the chat’s tool-call log shows each task together with its answer, because the model wrote it. The card your child sees reveals the answer only after they answer.

Your child’s data

It stays with you.

  • A file in your Google Drive. The profile, the ratings and the current task live in one file in a visible folder of your Drive. You can open it, and Drive keeps its history.

  • We keep nothing. The service stores nothing between requests — no profiles, no tasks, no answers. Its logs hold counts, never text or names.

  • A pseudonym, not a name. No real name, birth date or school. The pseudonym never goes into a task.

  • What the chat sees. The chat’s model writes the tasks, so it receives the task brief and your notes on your child’s level and tone.

  • Leave any time. Export the profile, delete the file, or disconnect MathTrail in your chat’s settings and remove its access in your Google account.

Honestly

What we don’t promise.

  • “Checked” means checked by a program: one correct option, the answer reproduced, readable for the grade, no repeats, a tidy drawing. Whether the wording, the drawing and the solution agree in meaning is left to the model’s own self-check — so now and then a task can be off. Press “Another task”.

  • No pictures. Drawings are made of text characters, so the topics are the ones that work in words: logic, counting, arithmetic with a twist, strategies and games.

  • Grades 1–6, olympiad style, no proofs.

Questions parents ask

Who is the user — me or my child?

You. MathTrail is used by an adult — a parent or a tutor — in their own chat account (Claude is for 18+, ChatGPT for 13+). Your child solves tasks next to you or with your permission.

Does my child need an account?

No. Only you sign in, with Google. The free version has one child per account.

Which languages does it speak?

Tasks come in the language of your chat — any language. Buttons and labels are translated into 22 languages and fall back to the nearest one, or English.

Why is this better than just asking the chat?

The chat still writes the task, but a program decides whether it reaches your child: exactly one correct answer, a solution reproduced by trying every case, the right reading level for the grade, no repeats.

What if a task looks wrong?

Press “Another task” — the next one is checked from scratch. If it keeps happening, tell us on GitHub.

How many tasks a day?

Enough for a lesson. Each account has a daily limit on new tasks, so the free service stays free for everyone.
