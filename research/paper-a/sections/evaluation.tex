\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:evaluation}

Both experiments run on the service's own code at the pinned commit, with no child and no language model, under protocols frozen and timestamped before their first runs; every number is produced by a script over saved data, and the deviations from the protocols are recorded with the artifact.

\subsection{RQ1: Fault Injection}
\label{sec:rq1}

\paragraph{Design.} We inject one defect at a time into reference tasks with their solvers, in classes fixed by the protocol. Those the checks must catch break a rule some check states, such as a wrong key, two correct options or none, a hard-coded solver, a hollow explanation or a copy; a few operators in them make a defect a child would see that no check is defined by. Those out of scope, the X rows of Table~\ref{tab:defects}, measure the limits of Section~\ref{sec:limits}. Apart from the operators that change them, every case keeps its task's reference solver and a self-check that finds nothing, so a wrong key meets a solver that computes rightly: a model's solver that computes its key's error is beyond this experiment. We report the share of cases refused, with \given{95}\pct{} intervals. False refusals of good new tasks would need tasks the checks were never tuned on, and are not measured.

\paragraph{Results.} Of the \stat{ea1}{hosts} reference tasks, \stat{ea1}{eligible} pass every check as they are: all \stat{ea1}{eligible_56} of grades \stat{product}{grade_level_3}, but \stat{ea1}{eligible_12} of \stat{ea1}{hosts_12} at grades \stat{product}{grade_level_1} and \stat{ea1}{eligible_34} of \stat{ea1}{hosts_34} at grades \stat{product}{grade_level_2}, where the others read too hard for their level or as near-copies of another, mostly in topics whose tasks share a stock opening, such as knights and liars. On these, \stat{ea1}{operators} operators of \stat{ea1}{classes} classes made \statnum{ea1}{cases} cases. Every case of the \stat{ea1}{mechanism_operators} operators that break a rule a check states was refused by the check expected\statzero{ea1}{mechanism_missed}: written from the checks' own rules, they test that the checks do what they state, not how far they reach. Every \given{95}\pct{} interval starts at \stat{ea1}{mechanism_lowest_low}\pct{} or higher, or at \statr{ea1}{drawing_lowest_low}{0}\pct{} for the drawing operators, which only the \stat{ea1}{drawing_hosts_fewest} or \stat{ea1}{drawing_hosts_most} tasks with a drawing take. All but \statword{ea1}{mechanism_operators_more_checks} of these operators were refused by one check alone, so the checks seldom back each other up.

Table~\ref{tab:defects} gives the rest. A copy of a reference task with every number raised by one was not reliably caught, refused in \stat{ea1}{d14b_refused_percent}\pct{} of cases, under the \given{95}\pct{} the protocol asks; a repeat of the child's own task with new numbers was refused in \stat{ea1}{d15b_refused_percent}\pct{}, every miss of either in one topic whose short questions are mostly numbers. A second correct option, the right number in words beside its digits, was never refused\statzero{ea1}{d02b_refused}: the solver matches an option by its text. Out of scope, copies with their people and objects renamed were mostly refused as near-duplicates: a child would read them as copies, while a plot written anew passes by design. The other out-of-scope defects went through, except where a check tripped over the changed text: a removed condition was refused in \stat{ea1}{x02_refused_percent}\pct{} of cases, by the readability check or the drawing check.

\subsection{RQ2: Simulated Learners}
\label{sec:rq2}

\paragraph{Design.} Simulated children go through the service's own rule, profile and update for \stat{ea3}{answers_per_child} answers each. A child has a true level, drawn around the start of a random grade with a spread of \stat{ea3}{child_level_spread} logit, and a true offset in every topic; a written task's difficulty misses the one asked for by a normal error of standard deviation \stat{ea3}{writing_error_sd}. \Statword{ea3}{generators} generators of \statnum{ea3}{children_per_cell} children take this population as it is or change it in one respect: a grade level off, learning and forgetting, a jump, a host writing harder tasks, linked topics, another slope or floor, hints. \Statword{ea3}{sweep_alternatives} alternatives run beside the service's update: its step with one level per child or per topic; a constant step and the likelihood-gradient step, with one level per child, per topic, or level plus offsets; Glicko-2~\cite{glickman2022glicko2}, Glicko-2 with the floor, and Urnings~\cite{bolsinova2022urnings}, each with one level per child or per topic. All rules meet the same children with the same random draws and the service's choice of tasks and mastery; the step rules keep the trial's placement, the others estimate from the first answer. A declaration of mastery is false when the child's true chance on the level's middle task was below \stat{product}{corridor_middle}. Each number is a mean or pooled share over a cell's children, with a \given{95}\pct{} bootstrap interval; a paired difference is found when its interval excludes zero. Four comparisons were fixed as primary before the run: the service against each baseline on children who stay put and on learners, the trial series on children placed a level off, and floors under the step on a jump; every other number is secondary.

\subsection{Cost and Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:cost}

The service makes no model calls, so a task costs it only computation: on a development machine, a review of a reference task took a median of \stat{ea4}{review_median_ms}\,ms (\statr{ea4}{review_p99_ms}{1}\,ms at the \given{99}th percentile), and the package the model writes from weighed \stat{ea4}{package_kib_min}--\stat{ea4}{package_kib_max}\,KiB. A task's time is the model's writing.

\emph{Internal:} our injected defects may be easier to catch than those models make, the harness ran outside any conversation, and the simulated children answer by the very curve the service assumes, which favours its update. \emph{External:} the reference tasks come from one model family and are in English, on which the readability limits were calibrated; simulated children are not children; the cost was measured on one machine. \emph{Construct:} catching an injected defect is not catching a real one, an operator can make text that is not the defect it is named after, and no check asks whether a task is olympiad-style or at the difficulty asked for. \emph{Conclusion:} each of the \stat{ea3}{primary_comparisons} paired differences in the four primary comparisons was judged by its own interval, with no correction for their number, and \stat{ea3}{primary_comparisons_found} found a difference, a few perhaps by chance. \emph{Search:} our claims of novelty rest on searches in English.
