\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

% The service's latest monthly snapshot of public totals decides the clause:
% no month counted whole yet, a month with too few children to show, or a
% month whose total is shown.
Children have used the service in real lessons, \ifcase\stat{live}{state} but its monthly public totals are still to come\or but too few in \statmonth{live}{month}~\stat{live}{year} for its public totals\or about \statnum{live}{learners} in \statmonth{live}{month}~\stat{live}{year}, \statpct{live}{correct_share}{0}\pct{} right on the rule's tasks against \statpct{live}{promised_mean}{0}\pct{} promised\fi, and nothing here measures learning; in the trials of model tutoring we read, gains came with correctness from outside the model~\cite{kestin2025ai}; whether automated checks can take that place for a child is untested. The seal hides the answer from the child, not from the model, an adult reading the host's tool log, or whoever inspects the card's memory, and what the model writes in the chat is not checked. Repeats are textual and remembered for the last \stat{product}{fingerprints_kept} tasks only, and the reference tasks are in English, so a translated copy is not caught. Difficulty is asked for, not measured. A false declaration of mastery is undone by \statword{product}{mastery_lost_after} wrong answers in a row, but a mastered topic leaves the rotation, so the correction waits until the topic returns. Most of the card's \stat{product}{widget_languages} languages are machine translations nobody has read.
