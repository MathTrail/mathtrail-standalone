\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

There was no study with children, and nothing here measures learning; in the trials of model tutoring we read, gains came with correctness from outside the model~\cite{kestin2025ai}, and whether automated checks can take that place for a child is untested. The seal protects the answer from the child, not from the model, from an adult who reads the host's tool log, or from whoever inspects the card's memory, and what the model writes into the chat is not checked. Repeats are textual and remembered for the last \stat{product}{fingerprints_kept} tasks only, and the reference tasks are in English, so a copy translated into another language is not caught. Difficulty is asked for, not measured. A false declaration of mastery is undone by \statword{product}{mastery_lost_after} wrong answers in a row, but a mastered topic leaves the rotation, so the correction waits for the topic to come back. And most of the card's \stat{product}{widget_languages} languages are machine translations nobody has read.
